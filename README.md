## Open Source, Privacy & Boundaries

Not every project needs to be public.

Think of me as a server.

Every server has finite resources: CPU, memory, bandwidth, and connection limits. I do too — time, attention, and energy are not infinite resources.

If every request is accepted without limits, eventually the system starts returning:

`429 Too Many Requests`

That is why I choose what to share publicly and what to keep private. It is not about hiding my work; it is about managing access to it intentionally.

In DevOps, we understand that rate limiting, access control, and resource management are essential parts of a reliable system.

**The same principle applies outside the terminal.**

Not every repository needs to be public. Not every request needs an immediate response. Not every connection needs unlimited access.

I share what makes sense to share, protect what should remain private, and invest my resources where they matter most.
